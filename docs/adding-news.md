# Submit news to Y-Matter Lab

**[Open the news submission form](https://github.com/ymatterlab/ymatterlab.github.io/issues/new?template=news.yml)**

1. Sign in to GitHub and open the form above.
2. Follow the examples in the form and replace the bracketed prompts in the news
   text template. Required: a short title, event date (`YYYY-MM-DD`), one category,
   and one to three sentences using full names. Optional: a full `https://` source
   link and an image with a description and caption.
3. Click **Create** to send your request.

Yuewen will review your submission and publish it on the website. If anything is
missing, he will ask in the comments on your request.

Submissions are public, so include only information and images ready to share.
To correct or withdraw a submission, leave a comment on the same request.
