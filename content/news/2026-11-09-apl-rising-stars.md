A paper co-authored by **Yue-Wen Fang** has been selected for the **APL Rising Stars Collection** of *Applied Physics Letters*. The paper, [“Control of magnetic transition, metal–semiconductor transition, and magnetic anisotropy in noncentrosymmetric monolayer Cr2Ge2Se3Te3”](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0276143), was published in *Applied Physics Letters* **127**, 092402 (2025).

The "APL Rising Stars Collection" highlights notable research led by early-career principal investigators. A selection committee drawn from the journal's Editorial Advisory Board chooses the featured papers.

Using first-principles calculations, the study explores how mechanical strain and electric fields can tune magnetism in an atomically thin Janus material, Cr2Ge2Se3Te3. It predicts strain-driven changes in magnetic order, a transition between metallic and semiconducting behavior, and control over the preferred direction of magnetization. These findings suggest routes to tunable two-dimensional magnetic materials.

The work was carried out by **Rui-Qi Wang** under the co-supervision of **Yue-Wen**. Rui-Qi received his PhD in **June 2026**.
